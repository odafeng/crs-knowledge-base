Conclusions

This phase 3 trial demonstrated that robotic TME resulted in a significant improvement in pathological quality compared to the control arm. This test paper was submitted to verify the functionality of the auto-merge pipeline. As the abstract provided is limited in scope, detailed quantitative outcomes, safety data, and comparative statistics are not available and should be interpreted with caution. Full results would be expected to include data on specimen quality metrics (e.g., complete mesorectal excision rate, circumferential resection margin involvement), perioperative outcomes, and long-term oncological endpoints.
